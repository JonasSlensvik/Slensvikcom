◉Data health & method

Every source runs on its own cadence on the slensvik.com server; each is isolated, retried with backoff, and validated before anything is written (non-finite values, implausible levels and isolated bad FX/index prints are rejected). What you see here is the live state of that pipeline.

≡Method

Moves & σ. Prices move in %, yields/spreads/policy rates in basis points. σ-move = the latest move ÷ 1.4826 × the median absolute deviation of the previous 60 moves (a robust standard deviation); a brief card needs ≥ 2.8σ. "Largest since" scans the full stored history for an equal or bigger move in the same direction.

Futures rolls. Front-month futures switch contract near expiry, and in a steep curve the switch looks like a price move (Brent's Nov→Dec roll on 28 Sep 2026 read −6.4% on a +0.3% day). Rolls are detected every run and divided out of daily moves, σ, volatility, correlations and spreads; levels stay the quoted front month.

Risk regime. Nine signals z-scored against their own trailing 252 days (clipped ±3), signed so + = risk-on, weight-averaged. ≥ +0.45 = RISK-ON, ≤ −0.45 = RISK-OFF; STRESS adds an absolute override (VIX ≥ 30 or high-yield spreads in their top decile of five years).

Tension. GDELT articles on fighting, assault and mass violence (CAMEO 18–20) as a share of all articles about a country over 7 days — shrunk toward the global share with a 2 000-article prior, mapped 2.5%→0 … 16%→1 — scaled by volume (log of violence articles per day). Spike = median of the last 3 days ÷ the 60-day median. It measures news coverage, not casualties: under-reported wars score lower than they deserve.

Chokepoints. IMF PortWatch daily transit counts (AIS). Status "disrupted" = the 7-day average at least 30% below the same week last year and 10% below the prior 90 days; "surging" is the mirror image.

Correlations use daily moves on a union calendar (holidays = no move); markets that close at different hours understate same-day correlation between Asia and the US.

Brief. A deterministic rules engine — no language model. Every figure in a card is read from stored data, so any brief can be reproduced from the database.
