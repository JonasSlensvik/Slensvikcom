◉ Credit cycle · Norway

Distress climbs down the ladder.

When money gets tight, households miss the unsecured payments first, then the car, then the mortgage, and companies go under along the way. Each rung is a public count of people or firms that could not pay, so a turn on one rung warns of the next, months before it reaches a bank's loss line.

§Method and sources

Rungs. Unsecured debt: Gjeldsregisteret's monthly key figures (interest-bearing balances — revolving or past due — since 2022). Liens: registered execution proceedings for unpaid debt (tinglyste utlegg, SSB 07218). Car loans: forced-sale requests on chattel (SSB 07218), which SSB stopped in January 2022 — there is no live car-loan rung yet; the candidates are the consumer-finance subsidiaries' stage 3 in the banks' reports. Mortgages: completed forced sales of dwellings (SSB 08948/11500, quarterly). Businesses: enterprise bankruptcies (SSB 07218), by industry from SSB 08551.

Readings. Counts are compared as their 12-month totals, year on year, which removes seasonality and smooths the monthly noise. Balances and prices compare their 3-month averages year on year. Unemployment (LFS, seasonally adjusted, 3-month average) is shown in percentage points.

Exposures. Each bank's loans-by-industry note (group), mapped to one sector list. DNB's financial row holds repo trading volumes; Sør-Norge's excludes loans moved to SpareBank 1 Boligkreditt and Næringskreditt; Møre states stage 3 as commitments (loans, guarantees and undrawn lines).

Banks. Figures are read from the quarterly reports on Oslo Børs NewsWeb. DeepSeek Flash reads every report twice, once quickly and once carefully, after it matched a hand-read test set of 14 reports field by field; each value is checked against the report's own totals, the bank's history and its peers. Five banks were also read by hand, and hand readings win.

Posted prices. Forbrukerrådet's comparison tool on Finansportalen, loaded every evening in a browser exactly as the public sees it. A bank's dot is its own brand's best standard mortgage (a flexi loan where it lists no standard loan) and its best floating savings rate, leaving out BSU, pension, green, lock-in and special-terms products. Every product is recorded, so a repricing is a change in a product's own price, not a new product taking the lead.

Caveat. The lead times are measured over four Norwegian credit episodes. They are a prior for when to look, not a forecast.

Data: SSB, Gjeldsregisteret, Norges Bank, Nord Pool, Yahoo Finance, the banks' quarterly reports (Oslo Børs NewsWeb), Finanstilsynet, Finansportalen (Forbrukerrådet). Refreshed through World Intel every 12 hours; the bank figures change when a report is read, the posted prices every evening.
