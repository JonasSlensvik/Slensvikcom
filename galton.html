❖THE CONTESTANTS · MODEL GALLERYEvery method produces a weight vector w each week on the same top-N universe and the same gross-leverage budget — they differ only in how (tail) risk is measured. Card outlines match the series colours in the chart below.

◇ Shared foundations

Fama–MacBeth Galton shrinkage. Each week, regress every firm's next-period moment cross-sectionally on its in-sample moment; the slope measures how much sample signal survives out-of-sample. The applied coefficient is the expanding-window mean of all prior slopes.
Min-variance closed form & the shared budget. Given any covariance Σ, the lowest-variance fully-invested portfolio is analytic — most cards just feed a different Σ into it (1 = vector of ones). The optimised cards solve w = w⁺−w⁻ with Σw = 1 and Σ(w⁺+w⁻) ≤ gross, where gross = Σ|w_GMV| that week — so leverage is held equal across methods.
↗GROWTH OF 1 NOK · LOG SCALECompounded weekly, gross of transaction cost (the feasibility panel below charges it). Three families compete: conditional-vol GMV (sample / EWMA / GARCH), higher-moment optimisers (semicovariance, modified-VaR, skew-kurt tilt, tail-inflated), and the fat-tailed CVaR model — against naïve 1/N and value-weighting.

△EXTREME TAIL · WORST WEEKThe realisation CVaR is built to shrink — bars are the single worst weekly loss, 1995–2019
GMV minimises variance and so wins the mild 95% ES (the body); minimising fat-tailed CVaR over a t-copula of skew-t marginals specifically protects the extreme tail the Gaussian is blind to.
⇄TRANSACTION-COST FEASIBILITYA high Sharpe is only real if you can afford to trade it. One-way turnover = ½·Σ|Δwᵢ| per rebalance, drift-adjusted so pure buy-and-hold drift isn't billed. The round-trip cost is charged on the full traded notional Σ|Δw| = 2× one-way. Toggle the assumed cost and watch the leaderboard re-order.

The CVaR LP rebalances hardest — its tail-driven corner solutions move every week. The conditional-vol GMVs (sample, EWMA, GARCH) and the tail-inflated covariance barely trade, because shrinkage keeps weights stable. That gap is what decides net-of-cost feasibility.

≣GENERALIZED-GALTON SHRINKAGE · EXPANDING-WINDOW CONVERGENCEThe Fama-MacBeth slope g₁ — how much next-period moment is predicted by this-period sample moment (0 = pure shrink to cross-section, 1 = trust the sample). Classic Galton shrinks only σ and ρ; the generalized model adds skewness and tail (kurtosis) channels.
